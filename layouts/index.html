Critical applied linguistics · Generative AI · Teacher education

Qinghua Chen

Assistant Professor, English Language & Literature Academic Group
National Institute of Education, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

I study how power, knowledge and subjectivity shape what language teachers and learners are able to do, and I build tools with teachers rather than only writing about them. Right now that means asking how teacher agency and translanguaging are being reconfigured as generative AI enters the language classroom.

陈庆华，新加坡南洋理工大学国立教育学院（NIE）英语语言与文学学部助理教授。研究方向：批判应用语言学、教师能动性、超语实践，以及生成式人工智能在语言教育中的应用。
