About

Current Position

Assistant Professor, English Language and Literature Academic Group, National Institute of Education, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

Research Focus

Grounded in a Foucauldian, poststructuralist approach to identity and power relations in educational contexts, my research examines how teachers exercise agency and how translanguaging pedagogies are reconfigured as generative AI is integrated into language classrooms.

Academic Background

PhD in Education (Language, Culture and Literacy) from Simon Fraser University (2022). With academic experience spanning Canada and Asia, I have published in journals including Applied Linguistics, System, Linguistics and Education, and Critical Inquiry in Language Studies.

Academic Philosophy

My approach to research integrates poststructuralist theory with practical pedagogical applications, bridging critical theory and lived educational experiences to contribute to more just educational environments.

Cultural Transitions

Exploring how identity is dynamically constructed through interactions with places, people, and ideas across different cultural and academic contexts, from classroom practitioner to researcher.
